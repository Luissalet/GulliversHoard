# Architecture

```
browser / MCP bridge / Faustus / Hoard Hub
        │  HTTP (127.0.0.1:5206)
        ▼
  main.py ─ api/ui.py (/api/ui/call, files, events, /api/models) ─ api/agent.py (Bearer, MCP)
        │
  agent_tools.py ── one catalogue of 20 tools (pydantic arguments)
        │
  services.py ── Settings · Sources · Projects · Jobs · area_check · export · Vulcan
        │
  jobs.py ── one worker thread ── worker.py (a child process per build) ── mesh/build.py ── outputs.py (STL, 3MF, tiles, GLB, thumbnail, report)
        │                                                                  │
  sources/ (http cache, dem, osm, catastro, aerial, geocode, fake)    mesh/detail.py (roofs, trees, arches, zones) · mesh/solids.py (manifold3d)
```

- **params.py**: `ModelParams`, the one validated object that decides a model (centre, ground size, shape, print size, base, exaggerations, resolution, layers, roads, contours, frame, label, tiles and pins, colour mode, elevation source), the three presets and the derived values (scale, model size, cell size on the ground, road rank by scale, automatic contour interval, tile size). `MAX_CELLS` (3 million) bounds the terrain grid.
- **geo.py**: a local projection around the centre with the WGS84 radii of curvature (error far below a millimetre of model for areas of tens of km); `Area` (centre + metres) and its boxes.
- **sources/http.py**: `Fetcher`, an httpx client with an on-disk cache (`data/cache/<bucket>/<sha>`) keyed by the request: Terrarium tiles and IGN rasters forever, Overpass answers 30 days, place searches 90 days.
- **sources/dem.py**: `DemGrid` (raster + pixel mapping, mercator or lon/lat, bilinear sampling) and `Composite` (fills the primary's no-data from a fallback). IGN: WCS 2.0.1 `GetCoverage` of `Elevacion4258_{5,25,200}`, split in requests of at most 1800 px a side, mosaicked, no-data below −1000; coverage chosen as the coarsest grid not coarser than the model cell (and at most 5400 px a side). Terrarium: the zoom whose pixel is just finer than the model cell, at most 144 tiles.
- **sources/osm.py**: one Overpass query (`out geom`) for buildings and building parts, water areas (with pools and fountains), waterways, highways, railways, green areas, trees and rows of trees, bare rock, scree and glaciers; servers raced (a new one every 6 s until one answers, three rounds). `parse` projects everything to metres, stitches multipolygon members into rings (outer united, inner cut), skips building ways that belong to a building relation (and `type=building` relations), reads heights (`height`, `building:height`, levels + half the roof levels, or the type), roofs (`roof:shape`, `roof:height`/`roof:levels`, `roof:orientation`), road widths (`width`, lanes × 3.3 m or the class), bridges and tunnels (railways too), splits the green into woods and parks, drops outlines whose 3D parts cover 60 % of them, and returns `Features`.
- **sources/catastro.py**: the Spanish cadastre's INSPIRE buildings WFS (`BU.BUILDINGPART`, GML 3.2 in EPSG:4326, no key): the area is asked in cells of 0.005° (a cell that returns the 5,000-part limit is split in four), four at a time, cached 120 days; `parse` reads footprints (with courtyards) and `numberOfFloorsAboveGround`; `to_buildings` turns parts into `Building`s (floors × 3 m + 1.2 m; small low parts are houses), `merge` keeps OpenStreetMap's 3D parts, mapped roofs and landmark buildings and drops the cadastre parts under them.
- **sources/aerial.py**: the PNOA orthophoto WMS of the IGN (`OI.OrthoimageCoverage`, JPEG, tiles of 2000 px at about 0.5 m/px, at most 64 tiles, cached a year); `canopy` = excess-green index (smoothed) above 0.03 and smoothed brightness below 112, opened; `trees` = crowns at the peaks of the distance transform of the canopy (thinned so they do not pile up), crown size from that distance.
- **sources/fake.py**: the invented town for `GULLIVER_FAKE=1` and the tests, in the Overpass format, laid out around any centre.
- **mesh/build.py**: `build(params, sources)` → `BuildResult`. Steps: elevation grid (`resolution_mm` cells, smoothed by about 0.45 × source resolution / cell), sea (≤ 0.3 m and connected to the border; its outline traced from the elevation with contourpy's marching squares on a grid padded 1 mm past the border), Z in mm (`base + (elev − ref) × scale × z_exag`, the exaggeration lowered when the relief would pass `max_relief_ratio` of the longest side), OpenStreetMap and (in Spain) the cadastre's buildings, roads buffered by their widths and closed (gaps under 0.36 mm filled, islands under 0.8 mm² filled), rails as their own outline, park paths (paths below the drawn road rank, inside the green), contour lines traced as lines (0.5 mm wide). The terrain solid (`solids.heightfield`) is then raised (raised roads, railway beds: the outline's prism ∩ a copy of the terrain moved up and shrunk 0.05 mm) and cut (engraved roads, contours, park paths in one colour: the outline's prism − a copy moved down and grown 0.05 mm), so every edge follows the vector outline. Water (`_water_levels`: a level grid, body by body: the lowest bare ground of the body within 150 m of ground, smoothed along the water and held under the ground around each point; a body whose level varies less than 0.25 mm is flat at its lowest point; the cut is the outline's prism minus the heightfield of level − depth, the water part the prism between that floor and the level heightfield, both on a sub-grid padded 1.5 mm past the outline; the sea one prism to a flat floor), bridges over water (`detail.arch_bridge`: a 0.5 mm deck on piers and semicircular arches about 28 m of ground apart, decks that touch share the higher height), buildings (cadastre parts or OSM; small ones kept when their block is big enough; simplified, grown 0.02 mm so neighbours overlap; walls from under the lowest ground point to the eaves and `detail.building_solid` roofs; parts with a `min_height` float on the parts under them), trees (`detail.plant` + `detail.trees_solid`: aerial and mapped trees, rows, woods and parks scattered when there is no photo; ellipsoid crowns sunk under the ground). Colour mode: each layer is the top 0.6 mm of the terrain inside its outline (`cutter = prism − lowered terrain`; `layer = terrain ∩ cutter`, `terrain −= cutter`), in priority roads › rails › snow › rock › green, each 0.08 mm off the ones before, off the banks and 0.03 mm off building walls; road and rail layers sit 0.03 mm inside their groove or bed walls; trees are their own part (trees − ground − buildings); a one-piece twin (`_single`) follows every later step for the STL. Then the outline shape (intersection with its prism), the frame (a prism around the outline; the copy used in unions reaches 0.05 mm inside), the text under the model (Pillow renders it, `mask_to_polygons` turns the pixels into polygons, mirrored), tiles (intersection with each tile's box) with pin holes (horizontal cylinders across each seam, every ~45 mm where the footprint allows) and the tile letter under each tile.
- **mesh/detail.py**: roofs (frame of the smallest rectangle around the footprint; gabled/hipped/skillion as `CrossSection.extrude` with a top scale, pyramids and cones to a point, domes and barrels from a scaled sphere or cylinder, clipped to the footprint when it is not convex), trees (jittered hexagonal scatter, rows sampled along lines, ellipsoid crowns), arch bridges (a profile in the along/up plane — deck, piers, arches — extruded across the road per straight segment), snow line and slope.
- **mesh/solids.py**: the heightfield solid (top grid with alternating diagonals, four walls, a fan bottom), prisms from shapely polygons (`CrossSection`, even-odd), outline shapes, text, tiles, pins, `mesh_arrays` (copies manifold's arrays, welds vertices that fall on the same float32 position and drops collapsed faces; the last 16 results are kept), `open_edges`, `unpinch` (a 7-sided rod 0.03 mm across subtracted along every edge shared by four faces: two bodies of one part touching along a line) and `clean` (unpinch, then bodies of no volume dropped, found as connected components of the welded mesh with scipy — manifold's own `decompose` is slow on parts with thousands of bodies).
- **worker.py**: `run_isolated(job, args)` starts a spawned process (`child_main`) that builds and writes the files with its own `Sources`, relays progress through a queue and returns the report; cancel terminates it. Used by the app (`Config.isolate`, on unless in demo mode or `GULLIVER_ISOLATE=0`); the tests build in process.
- **mesh/export.py**: binary STL; 3MF (core spec: `basematerials` with the part colours, one mesh object per part, an assembly object of components, metadata, thumbnail; vertices with six decimals, or nine significant digits when six would merge two of them); GLB through trimesh (nodes `<tile>__<part>`, simplified past 1.2 M triangles); the top-view thumbnail (layers, rock, snow, rails and paths on the ground colour, all multiplied by the hillshade so the relief shows through them; then trees + buildings with a shadow, cut to the outline).
- **outputs.py**: writes a build folder (`data/projects/<id>/builds/<build>/`): `<slug>.stl`, `<slug>.3mf`, `tiles/<slug>-<id>.{stl,3mf}`, `<slug>-tiles.zip` (with `LEEME.txt`), `preview.glb`, `thumb.png` and `report.json` (stats, parts, tiles with sizes, fit to the bed and their own check, file sizes, notes, attribution).
- **projects.py**: `project.json` per model (params, status, the current build and its summary, the builds kept), pruning to `keep_builds`; files are served only from the current build and only by a relative name.
- **jobs.py**: one worker thread (the build itself in `worker.py`'s child process); `Job.report(fraction, step)` feeds the progress and the log; cancel is checked between steps; `jobs.json` keeps the last 200 (running ones become `lost` after a restart).
- **services.py**: glue for the tools and routes: `make_params` (defaults + preset + values, or a patch over a stored model), `area_check`, create/update/build/delete, files, export to a folder, Vulcan through `family.call`, cache, and the family events (`gulliver.model.created`, `gulliver.model.ready`, `gulliver.export.done`).

Data folder (`data/`): `settings.json`, `jobs.json`, `projects/<id>/project.json`, `projects/<id>/builds/<build>/…`, `cache/{terrarium,ign,overpass,nominatim,catastro,aerial}`, `mcp-token`, `logs/`.

Interface (`client/`, React + Vite + Tailwind, the shared Hoard theme with the `gulliver` palette): `#/` library, `#/nueva` (and `#/nueva?edit=<id>`) map + parameters with the live `area_check`, `#/m/<id>` the 3D viewer (three.js, GLTFLoader, OrbitControls; Z-up model rotated into three's Y-up) and the report, `#/ajustes` settings. `copilot-context.js` is what the shared Faustus panel reads.
